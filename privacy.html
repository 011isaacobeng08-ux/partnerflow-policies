PartnerFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PartnerFlow ("the Bot") may collect, use, and handle information when used in Discord servers.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled, PartnerFlow may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, message or ticket information submitted through its features, verification submissions, moderation records, and bot settings.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate the bot, provide partnership and verification features, manage support tickets, prevent abuse, enforce server rules, and maintain service reliability.

3. Storage and Security

Information may be stored in the bot's database or service infrastructure. Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

4. Sharing

PartnerFlow does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized bot operators or relevant server staff where needed to provide bot features, handle reports, or maintain the service. Discord also processes information under its own policies.

5. Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the bot, maintain records, address abuse, or meet applicable obligations. You may contact the bot operator to request deletion. Some information may need to be retained where there is a legitimate reason to do so.

6. Third-Party Services

PartnerFlow operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own terms and privacy policy.

7. Children's Privacy

PartnerFlow is not designed to collect personal information from children outside what is necessary for its Discord features. Users should follow Discord's minimum age requirements in their region.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
